If comfort food had a gold standard, Adobo would be a top contender. At its heart, Adobo is a quintessential Filipino stew characterized by its perfect balance of salty, sour, and savory flavors. It isn’t just a dish; it’s actually a cooking method designed for preservation

Ingredients

  • Protein: 1 lb Chicken thighs or Pork belly.
  • Soy Sauce: 1/2 cup.
  • Vinegar: 1/3 cup (White or Apple Cider).
  • Garlic: 5 cloves, crushed.
  • Aromatics: 2 Bay leaves and a pinch of peppercorns.

The 3-Step Method

  1. Marinate: Toss everything into a pot and let it sit for 30 minutes (optional, but better).
  2. Simmer: Bring to a boil, then turn the heat to low. Cover and simmer for 30–40 minutes until the meat is tender.
  3. Reduce: Remove the lid and let the sauce bubble for another 5 minutes until it thickens into a glaze.
